OUR STORY: FROM THE BIN TO THE BIG LEAGUES

The Trash Can Hockey Club is more than just a collection of teams; it's a community born from a love for the game and a belief that hockey should be fun, supportive, and inclusive for everyone.

The Origin Story

Our journey began in 2023 at the Danbury Ice Arena's "E League," a developmental division designed for adult players—including those entirely new to skating. Under the guidance of coaches like Kyle Gonzalez and Cory Anderson from the Danbury Hat Tricks, our small, initial group grew steadily. Through dedicated coaching and a shared commitment to growth, everyone's skills improved, and a strong core group of regulars emerged.

After several seasons of development, we decided to take a shot at competition. We formed our first team, the Danbury Dumpster Fire, and joined the D League. Though we didn't win a single game in that first season, we cemented our reputation for heart and community. We never stopped playing, and our fun, supportive environment caught the attention of players across the league.

The Club Today

With our player pool still growing, we needed to find more ice time to ensure everyone could play. In 2025, we took a logical next step and expanded to a second location just 20 minutes away: the Brewster Ice Arena. This led to the creation of a second team, the Brewster Dumpster Fire.

As our presence grew and our reputation spread by word of mouth, we found ourselves with an even larger roster. The solution was yet another expansion, and so the Brewster Trainwreck was born, debuting in the Fall of 2025. Now operating three teams across two states, we gave our group its own name: the Trash Can Hockey Club.

The Story Behind Our Name

The history of our club is directly tied to the rich hockey culture of Danbury. Our name and logos are a tribute to the legendary Danbury Trashers from 2004-2006. As fans of the Trashers and the modern-day Hat Tricks, we wanted to pay homage to the team that helped put hockey on the map in the area.

The Dumpster Fire: Our Danbury and Brewster teams were named "Dumpster Fire" to honor the Trashers. Our mascot, Dumpy, is a hockey-playing dumpster on fire, which features the eyes of the Trashers' mascot, Scrappy.

The Trainwreck: The Trainwreck team name is a direct tribute to our roots. Named for the Danbury train station and museum located near our original rink, the name and its logo are a way for us to honor the city that helped us get our start.

The Club Logo: The Trash Can Hockey Club logo pulls everything together. It features a train pulling out of a dumpster that's on fire, with a garbage can holding a hockey stick and featuring Scrappy's eyes.

Our Philosophy

Our teams are built on the idea that adult recreational hockey should be an inclusive, supportive, and growth-minded activity that helps people build connections and find enjoyment in their lives on and off the ice. Looking ahead, we have aspirations of becoming a tri-state team and hosting our own tournaments, all while staying true to our core values.
