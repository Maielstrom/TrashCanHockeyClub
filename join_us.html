               A Team For Every Player            

           

               We’re always looking for new players to join our teams. Whether you're new to the game or a seasoned veteran, the Trashcan Hockey Club is a place for you. We are an inclusive community of players who are dedicated to helping each other grow, both on and off the ice. If you’re looking for a positive, low-pressure environment where you can develop your skills and enjoy the game, you’ve found your team.            

       
               
           

OUR ONLY REQUIREMENTS

           
                   
  •                    

    A POSITIVE ATTITUDE

                       

    Our club is built on a foundation of sportsmanship and mutual respect, and we ask that every player be a supportive teammate.

                   
    •                
  •                    

    WILLINGNESS TO GROW

                       

    Many of our players start with little to no hockey or skating experience. We welcome such players onto our teams and place emphasis on helping them develop their skills. It is expected that all players exhibit this supportive mindset and understanding of different skill levels.

                   
    •                
  •                    

    COMPLETE HOCKEY EQUIPMENT

                       

    Players are required to have a full set of hockey gear to participate in games and sessions.

                   
    •            
       
                      
           

HOW TO JOIN THE CLUB

                      
               

                   The process to join is simple and starts with our interest form. All submissions are automatically shared with our team captains, making it easy to track new players. Here's a quick breakdown:                

               
                       
  1. Submit our interest form through the button below. We only ask for simple details and information about your preferences, such as rink location or position.
    2.                    
  2. A captain will get in touch. They will reach out via email to answer any questions you have and provide updates about team placement.
    3.                
               

                   A quick note on playing fees. There are no fees or costs associated with submitting the interest form. Captains will discuss and convey playing dues for the season closer to the start of the respective season. Please note that such fees are only for playing/league fees; players are responsible for their own equipment and maintenance (like skate sharpening).
