THE GAME DAY VIBE

Game day for the Trainwreck begins in the locker room, where we review lines and discuss strategy for the night. On the bench, everyone is engaged and supportive as we try to help each other improve and grow. Between periods, we highlight exceptional elements of our play while also offering helpful observations to improve for the next period. We're all about constructive feedback and never place blame. Win or lose, we'll probably have a few questionable plays, but you can be sure we'll have a story to tell at the rink bar afterward.
