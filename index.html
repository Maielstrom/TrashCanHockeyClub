               TRASH CAN HOCKEY CLUB: YOUR NEXT HOCKEY CHAPTER            

           

               Whether you’re lacing up for the first time or a veteran looking for a fresh start, our welcoming community is built on camaraderie and fun, not on pressure.            

       
                      
           

OUR PHILOSOPHY

           

               The Trash Can Hockey Club was born from a simple idea: that adult recreational hockey should be fun. Founded by players who graduated from a local developmental league, we’ve built a supportive community that values camaraderie and good sportsmanship above all else. We welcome everyone, from new players just learning to skate to seasoned veterans looking for a low-pressure game and we’re dedicated to helping every player develop their skills in a positive environment. We’ve found that focusing on team spirit and a shared love for the game has not only brought us success on and off the ice but has also created a unique and positive environment that’s missing from other leagues.            

       
               
           

WHAT SETS US APART

           
                               
                   

OPEN TO ALL

                   

                       We welcome all skill levels, from those just learning to skate to seasoned players seeking a better/low-pressure environment. The only requirement? A positive attitude and a desire to grow yourself and others.                    

               
                               
                   

POSITIVE DEVELOPMENT

                   

                       We are dedicated to helping every player grow. Through our supportive environment you’ll feel confident working on your skills and playing for the love of the game.                    

               
                               
                   

WINNING CULTURE

                   

                       Trash Can Hockey Club proves that camaraderie and good sportsmanship are the ultimate win. We’ve built a unique reputation for a positive atmosphere, on and off the ice.
