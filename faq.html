                       <br><strong>For Skaters:</strong>

                       <h3 class="text-2xl font-bold font-bebas">Does the club have formal practices?</h3>

                       <span class="text-orange-500 text-3xl transform transition-transform duration-300 rotate-0" id="icon8">+</span>

                   </button>

                   <div id="answer8" class="hidden px-6 pb-6 font-oswald text-lg">

                       <p>The Trash Can Hockey Club does not have formal team practices. However, players often meet up for ad-hoc "sticks and pucks" sessions at the rinks. These sessions are a great way to improve your skills and play with teammates in a casual, non-game environment. The cost for these sessions is typically around $20 per person. Players will often post in our group chats to see if anyone would like to join them for a session. Occasionally, a group will also rent ice for a full-on scrimmage. These are not set on a regular schedule; rather, players will coordinate the session by first checking with the rink and then confirming interest via the club's chat.</p>

                   </div>
