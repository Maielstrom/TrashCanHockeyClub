               <span class="text-orange-500">The Can Commandments:</span> Code Of Conduct

           </h1>

           <p class="mt-8 text-2xl md:text-3xl font-light tracking-wide mx-auto font-oswald max-w-4xl">

               We are a low-pressure club built on a foundation of respect and sportsmanship. We believe that a few core rules are essential to keeping the game fun and safe for everyone. The following principles are a reflection of our commitment to a positive and inclusive community, both on and off the ice.

           </p>

       <!-- Code of Conduct Sections -->

       <section class="relative z-10 container mx-auto py-4 px-8 max-w-4xl">

           <div class="p-8 md:p-12 text-left rounded-lg">

               <div class="mb-10">

                   <h2 class="text-4xl font-bold font-bebas text-orange-500 mb-4">General Principles</h2>

                   <p class="font-oswald text-lg mb-4">

                       <strong>Respect for the Game:</strong> Treat every person involved—teammates, opponents, referees, officiating staff, rink staff, and league management—with respect. We are a sportsmanship-first club, and we expect a positive attitude on and off the ice.

                   </p>

               </div>


               <div class="mb-10">

                   <h2 class="text-4xl font-bold font-bebas text-orange-500 mb-4">On-Ice Conduct</h2>

                   <p class="font-oswald text-lg mb-4">

                       <strong>Keep It Fun, Not Fierce:</strong> The goal of every game is to have fun. Aggressive, reckless, or overly physical play has no place in our club. The leagues we play in are strictly no-check, and we take this rule seriously. We're all here for recreation and have work to get to the next day, so intentionally playing dangerously or seeking to cause injury will not be tolerated. We expect all players to be mindful of this, as well as being mindful of players with different skill levels. This also means that instigating fights or any type of aggressive physical altercation will not be tolerated.

                   </p>

                   <p class="font-oswald text-lg mb-4">

                       <strong>Leave It on the Ice:</strong> We play with passion and intensity, and sometimes things can get heated. While we will not tolerate any aggressive altercations on the ice, we expect that any disagreements between opposing players will remain on the ice and will not carry over into the next game. Once the final buzzer sounds, the game is over.

                   </p>

               </div>


               <div class="mb-10">

                   <h2 class="text-4xl font-bold font-bebas text-orange-500 mb-4">Off-Ice Conduct</h2>

                   <p class="font-oswald text-lg mb-4">

                       <strong>Commitment to Your Team:</strong> We use BenchApp to manage our rosters, and players (full-time or subs) are expected to check in as "In" or "Out" for a game no later than 4 hours prior to game start. While priority is always given to full-time players, this policy ensures fairness for our subs. If a full-time player checks in within the 4-hour window, and a sub has already marked themselves as "In," priority will be given to the sub. If you are unable to attend, please also mark yourself as "Unavailable" on BenchApp so there is a clear understanding of your status.

                   </p>

                   <p class="font-oswald text-lg mb-4">

                       <strong>Payment of Dues:</strong> All players are expected to pay their fees on time. Full-time players are responsible for paying their season/league dues by the payment deadline, which is typically within the first few weeks of a season. Subs are expected to pay their fees at the end of the season. Failure to pay will result in a player being ineligible to play in the next season, or until the payment has been made.

                   </p>

                   <p class="font-oswald text-lg mb-4">

                       <strong>Assumption of Risk:</strong> Player safety is a high priority for us, our leagues, and the rinks we play at. However, it's important to remember that hockey, regardless of the level of play, carries an inherent risk of injury. As such, all players participate in our activities at their own risk and assume all responsibility for any injuries incurred. The club, league, and rinks are not responsible for any injuries or related costs.

                   </p>

                   <p class="font-oswald text-lg mb-4">

                       <strong>Respect for All Skill Levels:</strong> Our club is a community built on growth and support, not on competition and blame. While we always play to win, a loss is a shared responsibility. We will not tolerate negative attitudes, blaming, or yelling in the locker room or on the ice. We expect all players to be respectful of their teammates' skills and abilities, regardless of the outcome of a game.

                   </p>

               </div>


               <div>

                   <h2 class="text-4xl font-bold font-bebas text-orange-500 mb-4">Enforcement</h2>

                   <p class="font-oswald text-lg">

                       Violations of this Code of Conduct will be reviewed by the club leadership and may result in disciplinary action, including but not limited to, suspension or expulsion from the Trash Can Hockey Club. Our goal is to maintain a positive and fun environment for everyone. By joining the Trash Can Hockey Club, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct.

                   </p>

               </div>
